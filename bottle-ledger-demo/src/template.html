Bottle Ledger Demo

A working model of the four-layer framework in the draft paper: tamper-evident NFC closures bound to excise stamps, a permissioned ledger signed by several agencies, the four VerifyScan checks, and risk-ranked inspections.

Illustrative model on synthetic data. It is not the paper's 1,500-outlet agent-based model.

Consortium peers

Every block needs valid signatures from at least 3 of the 5 peers. Each peer signs with its own key; here an HMAC stands in for the X.509/ECDSA identities a Hyperledger Fabric network would use.

What the risk model learned

What this demo does not do. It does not run a consensus protocol: blocks are signed by a quorum of peers but not ordered by Raft or PBFT, and the peers share one process. Isolation Forest and logistic regression are small JavaScript versions, not the scikit-learn models in the paper. The market is 100 to 600 outlets over 400 km by 400 km, with assumed parameters, so use it to see the mechanisms, not to quote effect sizes.
